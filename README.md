# 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes transactional sales data from an e-commerce company to identify business opportunities and provide data-driven recommendations.

The analysis follows a complete Data Analytics workflow, starting from data understanding, data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and ending with business recommendations.

The project demonstrates practical skills in Python, data visualization, and business storytelling.

---

# 💼 Business Problem

Management wants to understand:

- Which products generate the highest sales?
- Which categories generate the highest profit?
- Which regions perform the best?
- How do discounts affect profitability?
- Which products should receive strategic attention?

Without proper analysis, management cannot make effective pricing and marketing decisions.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

The objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Evaluate profitability by category.
- Compare regional performance.
- Analyze customer purchasing patterns.
- Measure the impact of discounts.
- Generate business recommendations.

# ⚙️ Feature Engineering

Three new variables were created.

### Profit Margin (%)

Measures profitability relative to sales.

### Discount Category

Groups discounts into:

- No Discount
- Low Discount
- Medium Discount
- High Discount

### Profit Status

Classifies transactions into:

- Profit
- Loss
- Break Even

# 📈 Key Findings

*(This section will be updated after completing the full EDA.)*

Example findings:

- Technology generated the highest sales.
- The West region recorded the highest revenue.
- Discounts above 30% reduced profitability.
- Some furniture products generated losses despite high sales.

# 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, management should consider:

- Reviewing discount policies.
- Prioritizing high-profit product categories.
- Expanding sales strategies in high-performing regions.
- Monitoring products with consistently negative profit.
- Optimizing pricing strategies.
